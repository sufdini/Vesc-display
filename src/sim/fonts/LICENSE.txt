The font files in this directory (Font16.inc, Font32rle.inc, Font7srle.inc) are
copied unmodified (only renamed from .c to .inc) from the TFT_eSPI library by Bodmer:
https://github.com/Bodmer/TFT_eSPI

TFT_eSPI is released under the MIT licence (see license.txt in that repo).
They are used here only by the PC simulator so that text renders exactly as
it does on the real display.
